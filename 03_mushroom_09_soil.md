# Ratio in soil
- Only soil is required for milk mushrom (no roots), to stand we use soil.
- 40kgs of black soil/banka matti/Jiguru matti, Calcium Corbonate 2 kgs,  8 to 10 kg cocopit(kobbari pottu) - 5:1, mix it without gaddalu. which is enough for 50 to 60 bags.
- Nallamatti is preferable over red soil.
- Add slight water like (kallepu) and mix thorughly with in 5 days this will become composte and mix it throughly again.
- The soil should be ready before 15 days, so we should start at the 6th day of incubation period.
- If possible keep the composte ready in prior (May or june), this can be usefull for an year gives more yield. should cover and preserve.
- The soil should steam before the day of casing
