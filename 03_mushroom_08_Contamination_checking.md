# Mushroom Incubation and Contamination Checking

## Incubation Period

The dark room period is around 21 days.

## Contamination — How to Check

- How does it occur?
- What type is it? Examples: Lycoderma, green mold, black mold, normal.

## Phases in the Incubation Period

| Phase | Days | What Happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Phase 1 | Day 1 to Day 5 | No visible changes; the bag appears to "sweat" and the spawn forms bubbles — this is the spawn learning stage. |
| Phase 2 | Day 5 to Day 9 | More contamination will occur during this phase. Overheating starts — do not add additional bags during this stage. |
| Phase 3 | Day 9 to Day 13 | Continued colonization. |

### Phase Details

Phase 3: No changes will occur in these days — just sweating, and the spawn will form bubbles (spawn learning stage).

Phase 2 (contamination days): Overheating will start. During this stage, keep bag/room temperature below ~30–32 °C;. More CO₂ will form; to avoid this, the door should be opened to allow cooling and fresh air. The door should be opened only when convenient (preferably morning and evening).

Mycelium development: Mycelium will form in white, then turn to gold, then to gold-brown.

If any green is a competitor mold (seen around 7–9 days), contamination may have occurred.

## Spawn Contamination

If green occurs at the spawn layer, the contamination is in the spawn bag only.

It will spread to all bags within a day.

Removing the contaminated bag immediately is the correct and necessary action. However, you must seal it before removing it.

## Water/Grass Contamination

If the spawn is white but the grass layer is green, the fault lies in:

- Salt content in the water, or
- Lycoderma (Dec/Jan/Feb) — may occur due to the use of green grass. Grass should not be used before 3 months after harvest. The 6-month crop starts in June/July and ends in Nov/Dec; leave the grass for 2 months to dry and use it after Feb to avoid Lycoderma.

If Lycoderma is seen, it will spread to all bags within 48 hours.

Water fault → expect widespread contamination (treat as a batch-level problem).

Lycoderma/grass fault → may start localized but spreads fast (remove individual bags promptly and watch the rest).

## Excess Water/Temperature Contamination

If the entire bag is green, the cause is:

- Temperature issue, or
- Excess wet content in the grass.

All bags from the same grass batch/room are at high risk, not guaranteed identical outcomes.

After the 10th day, there will be no green contamination.

## Key Distinction (for your notes)

| Symptom | Cause | Spread Pattern | Action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Green at spawn layer | Spawn bag fault | Spreads within ~1 day | Remove that bag; check spawn supplier |
| Green at grass layer, white spawn | Water salt / Lycoderma (immature grass) | Water → all bags; Lycoderma → fast (48 hrs), may start localized | Remove affected bags; fix water or grass age |
| Entire bag green | Temperature / excess moisture | All bags affected | Remove visible bags, but treat whole batch as compromised; fix temp/moisture for next batch |

## Second/Larva Contamination (Reverse Contamination)

Sudden appearance of only grass (mycelium disappears).

This contamination occurs around the 12th day, with some yellow spots. The larvae eat all the mycelium and form lakhs of eggs with orange molds, producing worms/insects within 24 hours.

check for flies/gnats in the room and use traps/screens, because if larvae are present, the source (adult insects) is in the environment, not just one bag.

May occur due to unhygienic conditions, keeping spawn in an unhydrated place, or bag formation issues.
