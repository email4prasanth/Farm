# Mushroom Cultivation – Technical Training and Learning Plan

The following training activities are recommended to gain practical knowledge before starting commercial mushroom cultivation.

## 1. Training and Learning Activities

1. **Local Farm Training:** Attend training sessions and gain hands-on experience from nearby mushroom farms to understand day-to-day operations.
2. **Seasonal Practical Experience:** Understand mushroom cultivation under different climatic conditions, particularly summer, winter and rainy seasons.
3. **Professional Technical Training:** Attend paid mushroom cultivation training programmes conducted by ICAR-IIHR (Indian Institute of Horticultural Research), Bengaluru and DMR.
4. **Farm Visits Across South India:** Visit established mushroom farms in Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Kerala and Andhra Pradesh to understand different cultivation methods and business models.
5. **Hands-on Cultivation:** Practise spawn handling, substrate preparation, sterilisation, bag filling, incubation, harvesting and packaging.
6. **Climate Management:** Learn temperature, humidity, ventilation and moisture management techniques.
7. **Disease and Contamination Control:** Understand common fungal infections, pest problems, preventive measures and batch recovery methods.
8. **Post-Harvest Management:** Learn cleaning, grading, packaging, refrigeration and shelf-life management.
9. **Marketing Exposure:** Interact with farmers, wholesalers, supermarkets, hotels and direct sellers to understand market demand and pricing.
10. **Small-Scale Pilot Production:** Conduct trial cultivation before investing in commercial infrastructure.

## 2. Additional Areas to Learn

| Area | What to Learn |
| --- | --- |
| Mushroom Varieties | Oyster, Milky, Button and other suitable varieties |
| Spawn Management | Quality selection, storage and sourcing |
| Substrate Management | Raw material selection and preparation |
| Infrastructure | Growing rooms, racks and ventilation |
| Production Planning | Batch cycles and expected yield |
| Cost Management | Cost per bag and cost per kg |
| Waste Management | Reuse of spent mushroom substrate |
| Business Management | Production records, sales and profit/loss |

## 3. Recommended Learning Approach

1. **Observe:** Visit local mushroom farms and understand their operations.
2. **Learn:** Attend professional training at ICAR-IIHR, Bengaluru.
3. **Practise:** Gain hands-on experience across different seasons.
4. **Explore:** Visit successful farms across South India.
5. **Experiment:** Conduct small-scale pilot cultivation.
6. **Evaluate:** Analyse production cost, yield, quality and market demand.
7. **Implement:** Start commercial production after validating the process.

**Important recommendation:** Do not depend only on classroom training. Gain at least one complete practical cultivation cycle, including harvesting and selling the produce. Ideally, observe more than one seasonal cycle before making a major investment.
