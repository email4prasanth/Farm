Prioritize accuracy, reliability, and long-term usability rather than choosing the cheapest components.

Do not recommend replacing the SHT31 with a lower-accuracy sensor unless there is a strong technical or availability reason.

Wiring Configuration
Use this initial wiring:

SHT31	ESP32 DevKit V1
VIN / VCC	3V3
GND	GND
SDA	GPIO 21
SCL	GPIO 22
The typical SHT31 I²C address is 0x44.

Check the actual module pin labels and documentation before connecting it. Power the sensor from 3.3V unless its breakout documentation explicitly supports another voltage.

Power the ESP32 through its USB connector using a suitable 5V adapter and compatible cable. Do not connect 5V directly to the sensor or ESP32 GPIO pins.

Hardware and Environmental Precautions
The mushroom cultivation room can have very high humidity.

Keep the ESP32, breadboard, and power connections outside the humid growing room wherever possible.

Place the sensor where it can measure representative room air.

Protect the sensor from direct water droplets, mist, and condensation.

Use a ventilated sensor enclosure that does not obstruct airflow.

Do not assume that a sensor's 0–100% RH measurement range means its PCB is waterproof.

Design the electronics enclosure and sensor placement separately.
