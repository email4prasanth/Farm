# On Milky Mushrooms

Tamil Nadu was once famous for milky mushroom cultivation, but not anymore — the reason is unknown.

## Spawn Varieties

28 varieties of spawn were developed, but only 3 varieties continue today:

| Variety | Description |
| --- | --- |
| CA | Beautiful appearance; 4 to 5 pieces |
| Microciba | Developed to address complaints |
| APK2 | Mostly running; commercially good — each mushroom weighs 100 g |

### Expected Outcome

One bag splits into 2 beds, each bed with 3 to 4 mushrooms. This is the investment (700 g), and combined they will give profit (3 to 4 mushrooms). Simple to look at and normal in taste.

90% of APK2 is used in the current Indian market.

## Complaints

- High contamination — may be due to substrate, spawn, or climate.
- Market complaints — hard, rubbery, medicine smell, elastic, etc.

## Spawn Process

1. Must wash hands with Dettol before opening the 1 kg sealed spawn bag.
2. Do not squeeze, twist, or crush the spawn in the bag.
3. Open and check the spawn thoroughly:
   - At the surface level, if yellow/black/brown spots appear due to transport, leave it for 1 to 2 days — it will recover automatically; otherwise, remove it.
   - Inside, if any green (contamination) spots are found, do not use it.
