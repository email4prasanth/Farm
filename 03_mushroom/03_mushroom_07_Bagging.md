# Mushroom Bagging

## Bag Size
Available bag sizes:
- PP covers with 120, 140, and 160 microns — per kg, we can get 60 to 70 bags.
- Packing covers with 60, 70, and 80 microns — per kg, we can get up to 100+ bags.

**Size:** 12 × 22

## Filling the Bags
1. Tie the rubber end at the closed end and reverse the cover.
2. Take a handful (20–25 g) of spawn and spread it at the bottom, at the center and around the circumference, then fill the wet grass up to 2 inches.
3. Repeat for the second layer, third layer, up to 7 to 8 layers; the total weight should be around 2.3 to 2.5 kg.

## Punching and Covering Holes
1. Clean the needle with Dettol using cotton before punching holes.
2. Preferably punch 3 holes vertically and repeat 3 times — i.e., 9 holes in total.
3. During winter or when the temperature is below 28 °C, cover the holes with sterilised cotton; during summer, no cotton is needed.

### Transport
- Bags with holes should not be transported; bags with holes covered with cotton can be transported.

## Overview
- 1 kg spawn, 7–8 kg dry grass (9–11 kg wet grass), 12 × 22 size cover, 1.5 to 2 inches of grass.
- For a 12 × 22 cover bed with 7 layers of spawn + wet grass — i.e., 130–150 g of spawn and 2.3 kg of wet grass — the yield is 6 to 7 bags.

### Grass Moisture Calculations

1 kg of bone-dry grass (0% moisture) reaching 25%–30% moisture:

- At 25% moisture: 1 kg / (1 − 0.25) = 1.33 kg
- At 30% moisture: 1 kg / (1 − 0.30) = 1.43 kg
